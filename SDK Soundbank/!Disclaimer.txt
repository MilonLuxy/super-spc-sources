There's no such a thing of SNES SDK released officially.
The sounds in here are based in how many times they reappear through the games from the SNES library, excluding the company exclusive ones.
The "SNES SDK samples" reffered here is only a catchall term, no release of the SNES/SFC SDKs contains such samples.
It's more likely that the SDK samples were provided by the SNES sound chip's manufacturer, Sony, or were shared amongst composers in the industry.
